# Paper status and kernel-result errata

`atomic-gdn2.pdf` and its matching `.tex` are the **16 October 2026 report**.
They are preserved as that dated version; the PDF has not been regenerated by this hygiene update.

Its model-quality results describe the current four training runs. Its kernel section,
abstract and conclusion retain earlier 468×/200.17× measurements and an older benchmark
model-size label. Those are not the current four-path performance results.

For current kernel claims use [kernel evidence](../docs/KERNEL_EVIDENCE.md) and the
[17 October benchmark](https://github.com/Akseleu-J/atomic-ops/blob/main/benchmarks/README.md):
411.75 ± 5.13× peak gradient evaluation and 117.76 ± 0.15× synthetic 34.87M LM step versus OLD;
34.50 ± 0.43× and 4.531 ± 0.004× versus the author's JAX_REF.
OLD E2E uses block rematerialization; the timed calls replay fixed initial state.

These numbers do not measure the larger trained models' speed. The updated combined
manuscript will replace the old kernel section rather than relabel old measurements.
